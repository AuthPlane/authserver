NOTE: Liveness/readiness probes hit /.well-known/oauth-authorization-server,
  which is a static metadata endpoint. It does NOT exercise the database or
  the signing key store, so a pod can be reported "ready" while its backing
  store is unreachable. Pair with PodDisruptionBudget + a real DB monitor.
